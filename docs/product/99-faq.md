# Frequently Asked Questions

> **Last Updated:** 2026-01-17

Common questions and answers about the Flexible Request Management system.

---

## General Questions

### What is this application for?
This application manages approval workflows. You can create requests, get them reviewed and approved, and track their progress.

### How do I log in?
Use your organization's single sign-on (SSO). Click "Login" and enter your corporate credentials.

### Who do I contact for help?
Contact your system administrator or IT helpdesk.

---

## Creating Requests

### How do I create a new request?
Go to **My Requests** → Click **"New Request"** → Select a Request Type → Fill the form → Click **Submit**.

### Can I save a request without submitting?
Yes! Click **"Save Draft"** instead of Submit. You can return to it later.

### How do I edit a submitted request?
You cannot edit submitted requests. Ask the approver to "Send Back" the request for editing.

---

## Approving Requests

### How do I know if I have approvals pending?
Check your **Inbox**. Pending approvals appear there.

### Can I approve on behalf of someone else?
Only if the approval is assigned to a group you belong to. Otherwise, no.

### What's the difference between Reject and Send Back?
- **Reject** = Request is denied, workflow ends
- **Send Back** = Request returns to requester for changes

---

## Groups & Teams

### What are groups?
Groups are teams who can collectively handle approvals. Any member can claim and approve.

### How do I join a group?
Ask your administrator to add you to the group.

### How do I claim a group approval?
Open the step in your Inbox and click **"Claim"**.

---

## Access & Security

### Why can't I see a request?
You may not have the required role or relationship to that request. Contact your administrator.

### What roles exist?
Administrator, Requester, Approver, and Viewer. See [Understanding Roles](./security/understanding-roles.md).

---

## Common Issues

### My request is stuck. What do I do?
1. Check **My Requests** for status
2. View the workflow timeline to see where it's pending
3. Contact the assigned approver
4. If needed, contact the Coordinator

### I approved but nothing happened.
The request may have more approval steps. Check the workflow timeline for next steps.
