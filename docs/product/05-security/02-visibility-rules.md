# Visibility Rules

> **Last Updated:** 2026-01-17

Learn who can see what in the system.

---

## Request Visibility

Your visibility of requests depends on your role and relationship.

| You Can See... | If You Are... |
|----------------|---------------|
| All requests | Administrator |
| Your own requests | Requester (creator) |
| Requests assigned to you | Coordinator, Step Owner, Approver |
| Request in your approval queue | Group member |
| Completed requests (if allowed) | Viewer |

---

## Draft Requests

| Who | Visibility |
|-----|------------|
| Requester (owner) | ✅ Can see and edit |
| Everyone else | ❌ Cannot see |

Draft requests are private until submitted.

---

## In-Progress Requests

| Who | Visibility |
|-----|------------|
| Requester | ✅ Can view (read-only) |
| Coordinator | ✅ Can view and manage |
| Current Step Approver | ✅ Can view and approve |
| Other Users | ❌ Cannot see |

---

## Completed Requests

| Who | Visibility |
|-----|------------|
| Requester | ✅ Can view |
| All participants | ✅ Can view |
| Viewers (if granted) | ✅ Can view |
| Others | ❌ Cannot see |

---

## Sensitive Data

Some fields may be marked as sensitive:
- Only visible to approvers and administrators
- Hidden from general viewers
- Protected during workflow

---

## Need Access?

Contact your system administrator if you need to see requests outside your normal access.
