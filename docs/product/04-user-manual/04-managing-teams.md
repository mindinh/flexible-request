# Managing Teams

> **Last Updated:** 2026-01-17

Learn how to work effectively with teams and groups.

---

## Understanding Groups

Groups are collections of users who can collectively handle approvals. Benefits include:

- **Workload sharing** - Any member can claim an approval
- **Availability** - If one person is out, others can cover
- **Role-based** - Assign by function, not individual

---

## Your Group Memberships

To see which groups you belong to:

1. Navigate to **Organization** from the sidebar
2. View the **Groups** section
3. Your groups are highlighted

---

## Working with Group Approvals

When a step is assigned to a group you're in:

1. The step appears in **everyone's** Inbox
2. Anyone can **Claim** it to take ownership
3. Once claimed, only that person can act on it
4. They can **Release** it back if needed

---

## Claiming a Group Step

1. Open the step from your Inbox
2. Click **"Claim"**
3. You now own this step
4. Complete the approval as normal

---

## Releasing a Claimed Step

If you claimed a step but can't complete it:

1. Open the step
2. Click **"Release"**
3. Step returns to the group pool
4. Any other member can now claim it

---

## Best Practices

- Claim promptly to avoid duplicate work
- Release if you'll be unavailable
- Communicate with your team about coverage
