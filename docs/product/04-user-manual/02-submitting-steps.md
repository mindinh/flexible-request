# Submitting Steps

> **Last Updated:** 2026-01-17

Learn how to complete assigned work steps in requests.

---

## What are Steps?

Requests have multiple steps that must be completed. Each step may require:
- Filling in additional form data
- Uploading documents
- Getting approval from a designated person

---

## Finding Your Assigned Steps

1. Navigate to **Inbox** from the sidebar
2. Look for steps assigned to you
3. Click on a step to view details

---

## Completing a Step

1. Open the step from your Inbox or the request detail
2. Review any instructions or requirements
3. Fill in required form fields (if any)
4. Upload documents (if required)
5. Click **"Complete"** to finish the step

---

## Step Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **PENDING** | Step is waiting to be started |
| **IN_PROGRESS** | Step has been claimed/started |
| **COMPLETED** | Step is finished |
| **REJECTED** | Step was rejected |

---

## Tips

- Review all requirements before starting
- Save your work frequently
- Add comments to explain your changes
- Complete steps promptly to keep the workflow moving
